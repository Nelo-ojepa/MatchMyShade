# MatchMyShade 💄

## Your Perfect Foundation Match, Powered by AI

MatchMyShade is an AI-powered mobile app that helps black and brown women find their perfect foundation shade instantly—no more guessing, no more endless swatches.

---

## 🎯 The Problem We're Solving

For decades, finding the right foundation shade has been a frustrating experience for black and brown women. According to a 2025 report by arbelle.ai, **53% of black consumers** say they always struggle to find beauty products that match their shade.

Shopping online means guessing and hoping you get it right. Shopping in-store means sitting through countless patch tests. Either way, it's time-consuming, expensive, and often disappointing.

**MatchMyShade changes that.**

---

## ✨ What We Do

MatchMyShade uses your phone's camera and advanced AI to:
- **Scan your skin tone and undertone** in seconds
- **Match you to accurate foundation shades** across inclusive beauty brands
- **Let you virtually try on** different shades before you buy
- **Save your matches** for easy reference when shopping

No more guessing. No more wasted money on wrong shades. Just your perfect match, every time.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### AI Face Scanner
Our proprietary AI technology accurately detects your:
- Skin tone
- Undertone (warm, cool, or neutral)
- Skin type

### Brand Database
Browse and match your shade across multiple inclusive beauty brands all in one place.

### Virtual Try-On
See how different foundation shades look on your actual skin tone before making a purchase.

### Feedback System
Rate the accuracy of your matches to help us continuously improve our AI and serve you better.

### Personalized Profile
Save your matches, favorite brands, and shopping preferences for quick access.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies We Use

- **Mobile Development:** React Native (iOS & Android)
- **AI & Machine Learning:** TensorFlow Lite for on-device skin tone analysis
- **Backend:** Node.js with Express.js
- **Database:** PostgreSQL for user data, MongoDB for product catalog
- **Cloud Services:** AWS for hosting and file storage
- **Image Processing:** OpenCV for camera integration and image analysis
